Reversibility Is the Ultimate Safety Feature

John Terrill · LinkedIn · /writing/reversibility-is-the-ultimate-safety-feature

Organizations obsess over preventing mistakes.

A better strategy is making mistakes inexpensive.

Prevention Is Not Enough

No review process eliminates uncertainty. No AI model is perfect. No senior engineer sees every failure mode.

The question is not only whether a change will work.

It is how safely the organization can recover if it does not.

Reversibility Creates Confidence

Rollback, snapshots, feature flags, staged rollout, and last known good states let organizations test assumptions without betting the company.

Reversibility turns fear into experimentation.

The Economics of Safety

If failure is expensive, teams move slowly. If recovery is reliable, teams can learn faster.

Reversibility is not merely an operational capability. It is an economic advantage.

The Point

The safest systems are not those that assume they will never be wrong.

They are the systems designed to recover when they are.
