The Future Infrastructure Stack

John Terrill · LinkedIn · /writing/future-infrastructure-stack

Today's infrastructure stack has compute, storage, networking, observability, automation, and increasingly AI.

A critical layer is missing: engineering judgment.

The Stack Today

Infrastructure provides resources. Observability shows behavior. Automation performs tasks. AI proposes changes.

But what coordinates intent, evidence, risk, prediction, approval, rollback, and learning?

The Decision Layer

The future stack needs a decision layer that sits above execution and below organizational intent.

It translates goals into reviewable, reversible, observable changes.

Why AI Requires It

AI increases the volume and speed of proposals.

Without a decision layer, organizations either over-trust AI or bury it under manual approval.

A decision layer creates structured participation.

The Point

The future infrastructure stack will not be complete until it includes a durable layer for engineering judgment.
