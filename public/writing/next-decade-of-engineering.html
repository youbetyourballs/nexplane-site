The Next Decade of Engineering

John Terrill · LinkedIn · /writing/next-decade-of-engineering

Twenty years from now, we may look back at today's engineering practices the way we now look back at pre-Git development.

Important work happened, but too much context disappeared.

What Will Become Obvious

Future engineering organizations will treat the following as normal:

  • Every significant decision preserved.
  • Every prediction measured.
  • Every assumption tracked.
  • Every consequential change reversible when practical.
  • AI participating in reviews.
  • Organizational memory searchable.
  • Engineering judgment measurable.

Today, many of these ideas sound like overhead.

So did version control once.

Software Is Not the Only Artifact

The next decade will expand what engineering organizations preserve.

Not just code.

Reasoning. Evidence. Intent. Assumptions. Predictions. Learning.

The Shift

Implementation will become cheaper.

Judgment will become more valuable.

The organizations that win will not simply generate more software. They will improve the quality of every decision that produces software.

Closing

The greatest engineering organizations of the next decade won't simply build better software.

They'll build organizations that continuously improve the quality of every engineering decision they make.

Software is the output.

Engineering judgment is the product.
