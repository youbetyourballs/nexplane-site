The Missing Feedback Loop in Infrastructure

John Terrill · LinkedIn · /writing/missing-feedback-loop-in-infrastructure

Most infrastructure workflows have plan, deploy, and monitor.

They often miss the most important step: learn.

The Current Loop

A team plans a change, deploys it, watches dashboards, and moves on if nothing breaks.

But did the change achieve its intended outcome? Were assumptions correct? Should future decisions change?

Often nobody checks.

The Learning Loop

A better loop is:

Reason → Predict → Decide → Execute → Observe → Revise.

The revision step is where organizational learning happens.

Why This Matters

Without a learning loop, organizations can deploy frequently without becoming smarter.

With it, every change improves the next change.

The Point

Infrastructure should not merely become more automated.

It should become more self-improving through preserved feedback.
