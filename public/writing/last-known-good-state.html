The Last Known Good State May Be the Most Valuable Object in Infrastructure

John Terrill · LinkedIn · /writing/last-known-good-state

Rollback is often treated as an operational convenience.

It is much more than that.

The last known good state may be one of the most valuable objects an infrastructure organization can preserve.

Confidence Comes From Recovery

Organizations innovate faster when they trust recovery.

If every change feels irreversible, teams become conservative. If recovery is fast and reliable, teams can test assumptions.

State Is Not Enough

A useful last known good state should include more than configuration.

It should include:

  • infrastructure state
  • application version
  • dependency assumptions
  • active incidents
  • recent changes
  • validation evidence
  • rollback procedure
  • owner context

Change Graphs

Infrastructure should understand how states relate to each other.

What changed? Why? Who approved it? What prediction did it make? What evidence followed?

Rollback becomes not merely a technical operation, but a return to a known decision context.

The Point

The ability to move forward depends on the ability to retreat intelligently.

The last known good state is the foundation of engineering confidence.
