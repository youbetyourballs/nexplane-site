Licensing

Open source. Every feature.

Nexplane is released under AGPL-3.0. Self-host it, inspect it, modify it — no feature is held back for a paid tier. Commercial options exist for teams that want hosting or support, not for teams that want more of the product.

AGPL-3.0

Open source — free to self-host

Every change type, every connector, every rollback mechanism — included. Run it on your own infrastructure, forever, no license key, no feature gates. AGPL-3.0 requires that if you run a modified version as a network service, you publish your modifications under the same license. That's fine for most teams.
